01 / Biography

About Me

Bridging technical textile chemistry with traditional handcraft and visual storytelling from Savar, Dhaka.

With more than 25 years of hands-on experience in the textile printing industry, I specialize in color preparation, chemical formulation, and traditional fabric ornamentation. Over the decades, my work has centered on achieving precise shade consistency, reliable print durability, and expressive surface design across woven and heritage textiles.

Alongside my professional craft in fabric painting, block printing, batik, and tie-dyeing, I create thoughtful visual stories on nature, rural life, travel, historical places, and educational topics through my YouTube channel, Nisorgor Chokhe—sharing calm, authentic perspectives with a global audience.
